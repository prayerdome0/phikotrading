Who we are

Good price. Honest work. Quality finish.

Phiko Trading is a construction and renovations company based in Chatsworth, Durban. From a single bathroom re-tile to complete builds and 2000 m² surfacing projects, every job is quoted by — and supervised by — the owner-director himself, anywhere in KZN and across South Africa.

The photographs on this site are our own projects: floor and wall tiling, luxury bathrooms, interlocking paving, tarred roads and driveways, and from-foundation-to-roof building work across the province.
